## Self-review

- Donor deletion is anchored by body text/table text, never paragraph or table
  indexes.
- Paragraph removal is defensive: it removes nothing if either anchor is not
  found, preventing an unexpected template change from deleting the remaining
  document.
- Cleanup is limited to the legacy principal/contractor equipment list, its
  donor cronoprogram/equipment-interference tables, equipment-specific sample
  measures, and donor welding sample block. Headers, footers, generic legal
  text, and unrelated tables remain untouched.
- Current Risk Master rows are rendered before the per-appalto sections,
  skip only blank descriptions, preserve valid rows, map current environment
  IDs, and use `Ambiente non disponibile` for missing mappings. An empty
  inventory renders `Nessuna attrezzatura registrata nel Rischio Master.`.
- The contractor `Tipo` / `Descrizione` table is unchanged and covered by the
  regression.
- `git diff --check` passed. The untracked `backend/pkcs11.txt` generated by a
  LibreOffice conversion attempt was inspected and removed; it is not included.
