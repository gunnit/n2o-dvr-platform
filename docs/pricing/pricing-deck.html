8  Channel conflict — the one thing that can break this

Selling to consultants and to the companies those consultants serve is a real conflict. Resolve it structurally, before the first contract.

  • Segment by size and risk class. Direct plans sell only to firms with <15 workers in low/medium risk classes. Everything above routes to a partner consultant — in writing, in the consultant contract.
  • Give consultants the override. A partner-referred company converts into the consultant's tenant; the consultant keeps the relationship and earns a 20 % revenue share on that seat.
  • Never publish direct pricing that undercuts a partner's engagement fee without saying why. €490/yr buys a template-driven document without professional judgement — a genuinely different product. State it explicitly in the marketing.
  • Legal exposure. The datore di lavoro signs the DVR and carries liability. Position the direct product as "DVR assistito" with an optional RSPP review — never as "DVR fai-da-te".

A manual full document set is 12–20 hours; a senior technician's fully-loaded cost is €35–55/hour. Lead the sales conversation with this figure, not the feature list.

3  Priced against what the market pays for less

Studio at €3.900 is roughly 3× DVR Suite Pro (€1.200, DVR only). The premium is carried by four things a side-by-side makes obvious:

  • 17 document types vs 1. Blumatica's own listino prices modules at €60–155 each; at their unit economics our attachment library alone lists above €1.300.
  • AI that removes data entry, not just formats it — SDS PDF → structured chemical record, site photo → equipment inventory, risk library → suggested measures. No Italian competitor does this today.
  • The digital sopralluogo app. The field technician captures once; office staff never re-key. This is the actual time saving.
  • White-label output. The firm's logo, P.IVA and RSPP name on every document. A desktop licence cannot deliver this.

4  Why "active companies", not seats, is the primary meter

Seats scale with headcount; revenue scales with clients. A 3-person studio serving 80 companies is a bigger account than a 10-person studio serving 20. Metering on active companies aligns our revenue with theirs, makes expansion automatic, and removes the incentive to share passwords. Seats remain a secondary meter so a 40-person firm cannot buy Solo.

A written annual smart-working risk notice is now mandatory and sanctioned — a document that must be reissued every year, for every company with remote workers.

The recurring value is "your compliance file stays current and defensible", not "we sold you a Word file". Price the subscription on that sentence.

4  Where the price ceiling is

Above ~€2.400/yr the buyer stops comparing us to software and starts comparing us to a full RSPP mandate with a human on site. That is a fight we should not pick — it is the consultant channel's business, and taking it triggers exactly the conflict described in Foundations §8. Multi-sede at €2.400 is deliberately the last tier. Anything larger routes to a partner studio.
