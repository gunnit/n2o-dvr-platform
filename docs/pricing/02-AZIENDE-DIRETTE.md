# Pricing Model B — Direct to the employer (single company)

*Buyer: a **datore di lavoro** who must hold a DVR for their own company and does not want to keep paying a consultant to retype it every time something changes.*
*Rev. 2026-07-23. Prices excl. IVA 22 %, annual commitment.*

---

## Positioning first, price second

This tier is **not** "DVR fai-da-te". The *datore di lavoro* signs the DVR and carries the liability — a tool that hands them an unreviewed document and walks away is selling them exposure, and it will eventually cost N2O its reputation with an inspection.

Sell it as **"DVR assistito"**: the platform does the structure, the calculations and the writing; a certified RSPP reviews and countersigns on request. That framing is what lets us charge above the €350 DIY box products and below the consultant, in the gap nobody currently occupies.

## Why these numbers

### 1. Anchored on the invoice they are already paying

| What they pay a consultant today | Market fee |
|---|---|
| DVR, up to 15 workers | €300–450 |
| DVR, PMI | €600–1,500 |
| DVR, high risk / complex | €2,000–3,000+ |
| Stress lavoro-correlato | €175–200 |
| VDT (6–9 workstations) | €400 |
| HACCP manual | from €200 |
| RSPP esterno, annual mandate <15 workers | €450–600/yr |
| Data certa (timestamp → notarised) | €300–1,500 |

A PMI buying a DVR + stress + VDT + data certa pays **€1,175–2,100** in year one and then pays again on every revision.

- **Base at €490** sits just above a single low-complexity DVR (€300–450) — but includes four attachments and unlimited revisions. On a 3-year view the customer pays €1,470 against €900–1,350 of consultant fees for the *same three documents once each*, and gets the whole attachment set plus every re-issue.
- **Plus at €990** replaces roughly €1,200–2,100 of first-year consultant work. It pays for itself in year one and undercuts on every renewal.
- **Multi-sede at €2,400** replaces €3,000–8,000 of multi-site consultant work.

### 2. Anchored against the DIY products already in market

| Product | What it is | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Easywork "DVR Generator" Safety Box | one DVR, DIY web app + printed manual | €350–380 one-off |
| DVR Suite Starter | 1 DVR/yr, cloud, 50 MB | €120/yr |

Both are single-document, no attachments, no AI, no calculations, no assisted review. **Base at €490 is 29 % above the Safety Box and 4× DVR Suite Starter** — a premium that has to be earned in the first screen of the marketing site:

- 5 documents in Base, not 1 (17 in Multi-sede)
- Real calculations, not templates: NIOSH lifting index, VDT exposure threshold, INAIL stress scoring, fire risk INF+SI+PI, PMV/PPD thermal comfort
- Autofill from P.IVA alone (VIES + Registro Imprese) — the company profile builds itself
- SDS PDF → structured chemical record; site photo → equipment inventory
- **Unlimited revisions**, which is the actual recurring obligation (below)
- An RSPP who will actually put their name on it

### 3. Subscription, not one-off — and the law says so

This is the argument that makes annual billing legitimate rather than extractive:

- **Art. 29 c.3, D.Lgs. 81/2008** — the DVR must be updated **within 30 days** of new equipment, an accident, a change in the production process or the organisation. A one-off document is stale the first time they buy a machine.
- **Art. 28 c.2 — data certa.** A DVR without a certain date draws a fine of **€1,228.50–2,457.02** ([Cass. 14579/2026](https://www.puntosicuro.it/pubbliredazionale-C-119/dvr-senza-data-certa-la-cassazione-fa-chiarezza-con-la-sentenza-14579/2026-AR-26559/)). Every revision needs a fresh timestamp — an annually recurring act.
- **Legge PMI 2026** (L. 11 marzo 2026 n. 34) — a **written annual smart-working risk notice** is now mandatory and sanctioned. That is a document that must be reissued *every year*, for every company with remote workers.

The product's recurring value is "your compliance file stays current and defensible", not "we sold you a Word file". Price the subscription on that sentence.

### 4. Where the price ceiling is

Above ~€2,400/yr the buyer stops comparing us to software and starts comparing us to a full RSPP mandate with a human on site. That is a fight we should not pick — it is the consultant channel's business, and taking it triggers exactly the conflict described in [00-FONDAMENTA §8](00-FONDAMENTA.md). **Multi-sede at €2,400 is deliberately the last tier.** Anything larger is routed to a partner studio.

## Segmentation guardrail (non-negotiable)

Direct plans are sold **only** to companies with **<15 workers in low/medium risk classes** (Base) or **<50 workers** (Plus). Anything above, anything in a high-risk ATECO class, and anything involving construction sites (POS/PSC) is routed to a partner consultant, who keeps the client and earns a 20 % share on the seat.

Without this rule written into both the consultant contract and the signup flow, the two pricing models attack each other and the consultant channel — which has 4× the ACV and a fundable sales motion — is the one that dies.

## Risks to this model

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Legal exposure if a self-served DVR is judged inadequate | Never market as "fai-da-te". Assisted-review add-on prominent. Terms of service state clearly that the DdL is the responsible party and that the platform is a drafting aid. Get this reviewed by counsel before launch. |
| CAC exceeds €320 and the tier goes underwater | Gate launch on a measured CAC from one paid channel test. Do not scale on assumption. |
| Cannibalises the consultant channel | Segmentation guardrail above, enforced in the signup flow, not just in the contract |
| Support load per euro is far higher than consultant tier | Self-service onboarding content, in-app guida (already built), 3-day SLA on Base. Watch cost-to-serve monthly; if it exceeds 15 % of ACV, raise Base to €690. |
| Buyer expects one-off purchase, resents renewal | Lead every marketing asset with art. 29 c.3 (30-day update duty) and the €1,228–2,457 data-certa fine. The recurrence is legal, not commercial. |
| No billing/quota enforcement in the codebase yet | Same prerequisite as Model A: Stripe + `Subscription`/`UsageCounter` on `Organization`. This tier additionally needs self-service signup, dunning, and plan-gating in the UI. |

---

## Recommended launch position

**Do not launch this tier first.** Consultant plans have 4–8× the ACV, a fundable sales motion, and N2O as a live reference. Build and prove Model A, then launch **Plus at €990** as the single headline direct plan (Base and Multi-sede as the flanking anchors), through referral partners rather than paid acquisition, with a hard segmentation guardrail from day one.
